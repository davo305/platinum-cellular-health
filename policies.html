21+ Access Requirement

Access to Platinum Cellular Health Labs is intended only for individuals who certify that they are at least 21 years of age.

Visitors are required to complete the website entry certification before accessing the main website.

02

Research Use Only

Materials, catalog listings, documents, and information identified as Research Use Only are presented solely for legitimate scientific, analytical, laboratory, and educational research purposes.

Not for human consumption. Research Use Only materials are not presented for ingestion or consumption.
Not for animal consumption. Research Use Only materials are not presented for veterinary or animal-use purposes.
Not for administration. Website content does not provide administration, dosing, injection, prescribing, or treatment instructions.
Not for clinical use. Research materials are not presented for diagnosis, treatment, prevention, or cure of disease.
03

Prohibited & Unlawful Use

Platinum Cellular Health Labs does not support, encourage, endorse, or assist unlawful activity.

Users must not use this website, its information, documentation, or research materials in a manner that violates applicable laws, regulations, institutional requirements, or product labeling.

No use for unlawful activity.
No misrepresentation of intended research purpose.
No use inconsistent with Research Use Only labeling.
No attempt to obtain information or materials through false or misleading representations.
04

Scientific & Educational Information

Information provided on this website is intended for research, laboratory, analytical, and educational reference purposes.

Website content does not constitute medical, veterinary, diagnostic, prescribing, treatment, or other professional healthcare advice.

Scientific information may evolve as new research, analytical methods, and published literature become available.

05

Certificates of Analysis & Documentation

Platinum Cellular Health Labs may display Certificates of Analysis, analytical reports, laboratory verification links, lot information, batch references, or other supporting documentation.

Original reporting: Laboratory reports should be displayed without altering the reported analytical results.
Lot matching: A report should only be represented as applying to the sample, product, lot, or batch identified by the available documentation.
External verification: Where a testing laboratory provides an online verification page, an external link may be provided.
Historical documentation: Testing records may relate to a particular sample or batch and should not automatically be assumed to represent every future batch.
06

Right to Restrict or Refuse Service

Platinum Cellular Health Labs reserves the right to restrict website access, decline service, cancel activity, or refuse a transaction where appropriate.

This may include circumstances involving suspected misuse, unlawful activity, misrepresentation, fraud, abuse, or use inconsistent with Research Use Only labeling.

07

Privacy & Information Collection

Platinum Cellular Health Labs should collect only information reasonably necessary for website administration, communication, security, documentation, and other legitimate site functions.

Sensitive information should not be displayed publicly unless there is a legitimate reason and appropriate protection.

A more detailed Privacy Policy can be added when account registration, email collection, forms, or other data-processing features are activated.

08

Policy Updates

Website policies may be revised as the website, research library, documentation system, or applicable requirements change.

Continued use of the website after updated policies are published may be treated as acceptance of the revised terms where permitted by applicable law.
