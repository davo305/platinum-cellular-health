SHIPPING & PROCESSING POLICY

Customer-selected shipping with fast processing.

Normal Processing Time

Orders are normally prepared for shipment within 24 hours after the order is placed and payment is confirmed. This is a normal processing target rather than an absolute guarantee.

Processing Exceptions

Processing may occasionally take longer because of weekends, holidays, inventory verification, carrier availability, severe weather, operational interruptions, or other circumstances.

Shipping Charges

Shipping generally ranges from approximately $15 to $60 depending on the shipping method selected by the customer, destination, carrier, and available delivery service.

Customer Selects Shipping

Customers may select from the shipping options made available for the order. Faster, premium, or specialized shipping services may carry higher charges.

Optional Shipping Insurance

Customers may choose to add shipping insurance for an additional charge of $1 per $100 of order value. Insurance is optional and should be selected before the shipment is processed.

Insurance Example

A $100 order may carry a $1 optional insurance charge, a $300 order may carry a $3 charge, and a $500 order may carry a $5 charge.

Insurance Coverage

Where insurance is selected, coverage, exclusions, claim procedures, documentation requirements, and reimbursement are subject to the terms applicable to the carrier or insurance provider.

Final Shipping Cost

The applicable shipping charge and any selected insurance charge should be disclosed before payment is completed.

Delivery Information

Customers are responsible for supplying an accurate and deliverable shipping address and should carefully review all address information before completing the order.

Tracking

When tracking information is available for the selected shipping service, it may be provided after the shipment has been processed.

Carrier Delays

Weather, holidays, carrier disruptions, transportation conditions, and other circumstances outside PCH Labs' direct control may affect delivery after a shipment has been transferred to the carrier.

Unshipped Orders

Where applicable law requires cancellation, delay consent, or a refund because merchandise cannot be shipped within the required or promised time, PCH Labs will handle the order in accordance with those requirements.

ANALYTICAL DOCUMENTATION

Certificate of Analysis policy.

Available analytical documentation is provided to assist with research recordkeeping and sample or batch identification.

Batch-Specific Results

A Certificate of Analysis applies to the sample, lot, batch, or laboratory submission identified within the original analytical report.

No Universal Representation

A laboratory result for one tested sample should not automatically be interpreted as a result for another batch, lot, future production run, or different research material.

Original Report Controls

Website summaries are provided for convenience. If there is a difference between a website summary and an original laboratory report, researchers should rely on the original report.

Independent Laboratories

Where reports were issued by independent analytical laboratories, the laboratory is responsible for its own testing methods, measurements, reporting, and statements contained in the report.

Purity does not equal every quality attribute.

Identity, chromatographic purity, quantity, sterility, endotoxin, and other analytical measurements address different scientific questions.

Always review the full report.

Test methodology, identifiers, dates, laboratory notes, and reported limitations can provide important context for analytical results.
