PAYMENT & ACCOUNT SECURITY

Protect your wallet and account information.

PCH Labs accepts USDC on the Solana network or SOL. Cryptocurrency payments require careful verification because blockchain transactions may be irreversible after confirmation.

Never Send Your Seed Phrase

PCH Labs will never ask for your cryptocurrency wallet seed phrase, recovery phrase, or private key.

Verify the Solana Network

When paying with USDC, confirm that the transaction is using the Solana network before sending payment.

Verify the Wallet Address

Confirm the recipient address and payment amount before transmitting cryptocurrency.

BEFORE CONTACTING SUPPORT

Help us locate your request quickly.

Providing the relevant non-sensitive information in your first message can make it easier to understand and respond to your inquiry.

Order Questions

Include your name, order reference if available, and a brief description of the question. Do not include wallet private keys or seed phrases.

Shipping Questions

Include the order reference and tracking number, if one has already been provided.

COA Questions

Include the research-material name, amount, and batch or lot information when available.

Payment Questions

Include only the information needed to identify the transaction. Never provide wallet credentials.

Catalog Questions

Reference the exact research-material name and listed amount shown in the catalog.

Policy Questions

Review the Policies page for payment, shipping, final-sale, documentation, and research-use terms.
