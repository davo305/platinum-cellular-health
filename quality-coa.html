What to look for on a COA

A Certificate of Analysis should be reviewed as an analytical document. Important identifiers help determine which sample or lot the report actually represents.

✓ Product or sample identification
✓ Lot, batch, or sample number
✓ Laboratory name
✓ Testing date
✓ Analytical method
✓ Reported analytical results

Documentation Integrity

Certificates of Analysis and laboratory reports should be displayed as issued by the testing laboratory. Analytical results should not be altered, and a report should not be represented as applying to a different product, sample, lot, or batch than the one identified in the original documentation.
