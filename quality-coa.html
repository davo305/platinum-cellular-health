IMPORTANT COA NOTICE

Certificates of Analysis remain in their original form and apply only to the sample, lot, batch, or laboratory submission identified in the specific report. Multiple reports are kept separately where available. Endotoxin reports are displayed as separate analytical documentation.

DOCUMENT REVIEW

What to look for on a COA.

Analytical reports may contain different information depending on the laboratory and testing method. These are some of the most useful identifiers to review.

Sample IdentityThe name or identifier assigned to the submitted sample.
Lot / BatchInformation used to associate documentation with the corresponding research material.
Testing DateThe date the sample was analyzed or the report was issued.
LaboratoryThe analytical laboratory responsible for performing and reporting the test.
